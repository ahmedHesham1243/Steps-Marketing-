01How entry works

Four decisions. We stay for all of them.

Each step ends with something you can hold: a decision, a registration, a signed partner, a demand plan.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Feasibility, channel map, competitor benchmark and the regulatory landscape for your category.

    You geta go or no-go call and a go-to-market roadmap.

  2. 02

    Set up

    SFDA registration guidance, the right importer or entity structure, and pricing built for Saudi trade, with specialist partners where needed.

    You geta product that is allowed, and priced, to sell.

  3. 03

    Enter

    Introductions to established distributors, wholesalers and industrial buyers across the Eastern, Central and Western regions, with terms built for the long run.

    You getsigned channel partners, not a single shipment.

  4. 04

    Grow

    Positioning, B2B campaigns, content, and HORECA and retail visibility, calibrated for Saudi trade and consumer dynamics.

    You getdemand that sustains sell-through.
